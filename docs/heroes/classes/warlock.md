# Warlock

## Resource

Mana

## Specializations

| Specialization | Role Direction | Auto-Attack Direction | Primary Stat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Affliction | Damage over time | Shadow spell auto-attack | Intellect |
| Demonology | Summon / pet-focused spell damage | Demonic spell auto-attack | Intellect |
| Destruction | Direct spell damage | Destructive spell auto-attack | Intellect |

## Talent Trees

Warlock has three talent trees:

- Affliction
- Demonology
- Destruction

Each tree follows the shared 3-tier structure in [Classes](./README.md): 3 Tier 1 choices, 3 Tier 2 choices, and 2 mutually exclusive Tier 3 capstones.

## Combat Loadout

A Warlock equips:

- the auto-attack defined by the active specialization
- exactly 2 selected cooldown abilities
- exactly 1 selected ultimate

Warlock abilities spend Mana when they have a resource cost.

Pet and summon rules remain open.
