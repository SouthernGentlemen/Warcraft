# Hunter

## Resource

Mana

## Specializations

| Specialization | Role Direction | Auto-Attack Direction | Primary Stat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Beast Mastery | Ranged damage / pet support | Ranged weapon shot | Agility |
| Marksmanship | Direct ranged damage | Precision ranged weapon shot | Agility |
| Survival | Ranged damage / utility | Tactical ranged weapon shot | Agility |

## Talent Trees

Hunter has three talent trees:

- Beast Mastery
- Marksmanship
- Survival

Each tree follows the shared 3-tier structure in [Classes](./README.md): 3 Tier 1 choices, 3 Tier 2 choices, and 2 mutually exclusive Tier 3 capstones.

## Combat Loadout

A Hunter equips:

- the auto-attack defined by the active specialization
- exactly 2 selected cooldown abilities
- exactly 1 selected ultimate

Hunter abilities spend Mana when they have a resource cost.

Pet rules for Beast Mastery remain open.
