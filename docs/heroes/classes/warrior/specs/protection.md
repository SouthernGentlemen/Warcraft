# Warrior — Protection

## Identity

- Role: Tank / defense
- Resource: Rage
- Primary stat: Strength
- Auto-attack: Guarded Strike — defensive melee weapon damage

## Talent Tree

The first talent point spent in this tree selects **Protection** as the hero's specialization.

All values below are starting implementation values and may be tuned during balance passes.

### Tier 1 — Level 1

Choose 1 of 3.

| Talent | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Bulwark** | Gain 10% more Stamina from equipped gear. |
| **Anger Management** | Taking direct damage generates 3 Rage; can trigger once per second. |
| **Guarded Assault** | Auto-attacks grant a shield equal to 3% maximum health for 4 seconds. |

### Tier 2 — Level 2

Requires a Tier 1 talent in this tree. Choose 1 of 3.

| Talent | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Revenge** | After taking direct damage, the next auto-attack deals 40% additional damage; 6-second internal cooldown. |
| **Last Stand** | Once per combat when falling below 30% health, gain a shield equal to 25% maximum health. |
| **Hold the Line** | Using an equipped cooldown grants 10% damage reduction for 3 seconds. |

### Tier 3 — Level 3 Capstone

Requires a Tier 2 talent in this tree. Choose exactly 1 of 2.

| Capstone | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Fortress** | While above 50% health, take 15% less damage; while below 50% health, generate 25% more Rage. |
| **Avatar** | Using the selected ultimate increases maximum health by 20% and grants 20% damage reduction for 8 seconds. |

Selecting a capstone completes the primary specialization path and unlocks the class's other two talent trees.

## Combat Loadout

This specialization uses its listed auto-attack, exactly 2 equipped cooldown abilities, and exactly 1 equipped ultimate.

Talents modify that shared combat package; they do not add extra active ability slots.
