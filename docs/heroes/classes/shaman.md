# Shaman

## Faction

Horde only.

## Resource

Mana

## Specializations

| Specialization | Role Direction | Auto-Attack Direction | Primary Stat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Elemental | Spell damage | Elemental spell auto-attack | Intellect |
| Enhancement | Melee damage | Elemental melee strike | Agility |
| Restoration | Healing | Restoration spell auto-attack | Intellect |

## Talent Trees

Shaman has three talent trees:

- Elemental
- Enhancement
- Restoration

Each tree follows the shared 3-tier structure in [Classes](./README.md): 3 Tier 1 choices, 3 Tier 2 choices, and 2 mutually exclusive Tier 3 capstones.

## Combat Loadout

A Shaman equips:

- the auto-attack defined by the active specialization
- exactly 2 selected cooldown abilities
- exactly 1 selected ultimate

Shaman abilities spend Mana when they have a resource cost.

## Open Areas
