# Paladin — Retribution

## Identity

- Role: Holy melee damage
- Resource: Mana
- Primary stat: Strength
- Auto-attack: Crusader Strike — melee Holy weapon damage

## Talent Tree

The first talent point spent in this tree selects **Retribution** as the hero's specialization.

All values below are starting implementation values and may be tuned during balance passes.

### Tier 1 — Level 1

Choose 1 of 3.

| Talent | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Seal of Command** | Every fourth Crusader Strike immediately triggers an additional strike for 50% damage. |
| **Crusade** | Crusader Strike deals 12% more damage. |
| **Sanctified Efficiency** | Mana costs of equipped cooldown abilities are reduced by 10%. |

### Tier 2 — Level 2

Requires a Tier 1 talent in this tree. Choose 1 of 3.

| Talent | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Judgment of Light** | Critical auto-attacks heal the lowest-health ally for 10% of damage dealt. |
| **Sanctified Wrath** | Critical damage generates 5% additional ultimate-bar charge. |
| **Divine Purpose** | Using an equipped cooldown has a 10% chance to make the next equipped cooldown cost no Mana and deal 15% more damage. |

### Tier 3 — Level 3 Capstone

Requires a Tier 2 talent in this tree. Choose exactly 1 of 2.

| Capstone | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Avenging Wrath** | Using the selected ultimate grants 25% Attack Power and 15% Critical Strike chance for 8 seconds. |
| **Divine Storm** | Every fifth Crusader Strike also hits all other enemies for 60% damage. |

Selecting a capstone completes the primary specialization path and unlocks the class's other two talent trees.

## Combat Loadout

This specialization uses its listed auto-attack, exactly 2 equipped cooldown abilities, and exactly 1 equipped ultimate.

Talents modify that shared combat package; they do not add extra active ability slots.
