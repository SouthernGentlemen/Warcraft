# Hunter — Marksmanship

## Identity

- Role: Direct ranged weapon damage
- Resource: Mana
- Primary stat: Agility
- Auto-attack: Precision Shot — ranged weapon damage

## Talent Tree

The first talent point spent in this tree selects **Marksmanship** as the hero's specialization.

All values below are starting implementation values and may be tuned during balance passes.

### Tier 1 — Level 1

Choose 1 of 3.

| Talent | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Careful Aim** | Precision Shot deals 20% more damage to targets above 70% health. |
| **Quickdraw** | Precision Shot's auto-attack bar fills 10% faster. |
| **Efficient Quiver** | Mana costs of equipped cooldown abilities are reduced by 10%. |

### Tier 2 — Level 2

Requires a Tier 1 talent in this tree. Choose 1 of 3.

| Talent | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Piercing Shots** | Critical Precision Shots bleed the target for 20% of the hit's damage over 4 seconds. |
| **Lock and Load** | Using an equipped cooldown has a 15% chance to reset the other equipped cooldown. |
| **Sniper Training** | After two consecutive auto-attacks against the same target, gain 10% Critical Strike chance and 10% more Hit Rating until changing targets. |

### Tier 3 — Level 3 Capstone

Requires a Tier 2 talent in this tree. Choose exactly 1 of 2.

| Capstone | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Trueshot** | Using the selected ultimate grants 30% auto-attack speed and 20% more Hit Rating for 8 seconds. |
| **Kill Shot** | Deal 30% more damage to targets below 30% health. |

Selecting a capstone completes the primary specialization path and unlocks the class's other two talent trees.

## Combat Loadout

This specialization uses its listed auto-attack, exactly 2 equipped cooldown abilities, and exactly 1 equipped ultimate.

Talents modify that shared combat package; they do not add extra active ability slots.
