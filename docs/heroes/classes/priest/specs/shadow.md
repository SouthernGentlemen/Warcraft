# Priest — Shadow

## Identity

- Role: Ranged Shadow damage / sustain
- Resource: Mana
- Primary stat: Intellect
- Auto-attack: Shadow Bolt — ranged Shadow damage

## Talent Tree

The first talent point spent in this tree selects **Shadow** as the hero's specialization.

All values below are starting implementation values and may be tuned during balance passes.

### Tier 1 — Level 1

Choose 1 of 3.

| Talent | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Shadow Word: Pain** | Shadow Bolt applies a damage-over-time effect for 12% of the hit's damage over 6 seconds. |
| **Dark Insight** | Damage-over-time ticks have a 10% chance to advance the auto-attack bar by 20%. |
| **Vampiric Embrace** | 10% of Shadow damage dealt heals the lowest-health ally. |

### Tier 2 — Level 2

Requires a Tier 1 talent in this tree. Choose 1 of 3.

| Talent | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Mind Melt** | Equipped cooldown abilities deal 15% more damage to targets affected by Shadow Word: Pain. |
| **Misery** | Shadow Word: Pain lasts 30% longer. |
| **Shadow Focus** | Mana costs of equipped cooldown abilities are reduced by 12%. |

### Tier 3 — Level 3 Capstone

Requires a Tier 2 talent in this tree. Choose exactly 1 of 2.

| Capstone | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Voidform** | Using the selected ultimate grants 25% Spell Power and 20% Haste for 8 seconds. |
| **Devouring Plague** | Shadow Word: Pain can stack twice, and 15% of its damage heals this hero. |

Selecting a capstone completes the primary specialization path and unlocks the class's other two talent trees.

## Combat Loadout

This specialization uses its listed auto-attack, exactly 2 equipped cooldown abilities, and exactly 1 equipped ultimate.

Talents modify that shared combat package; they do not add extra active ability slots.
