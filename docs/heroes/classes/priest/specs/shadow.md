# Priest — Shadow

## Identity

- Role direction: Ranged spell damage
- Resource: Mana
- Primary stat direction: Intellect
- Auto-attack: Shadow Bolt

## Spec Theme

Shadow damage, damage-over-time pressure, self-sustain, debuffs.

This document defines the specialization's identity and design direction. Exact numbers and complete ability lists are intentionally deferred.

## Auto-Attack

The specialization uses **Shadow Bolt** as its spec-defined repeating auto-attack.

The attack resolves whenever the shared real-time auto-attack bar fills.

Exact attack time, coefficient, damage school, targeting, and weapon interaction remain open.

## Talent Tree

The Shadow tree contains:

- Tier 1 — 3 options
- Tier 2 — 3 options
- Tier 3 — 2 mutually exclusive capstones

If the hero's first talent point is spent in this tree, Shadow becomes the hero's specialization.

The hero must progress Tier 1 -> Tier 2 -> one Tier 3 capstone before the other two Priest trees unlock.

## Talent Direction

Talent options in this tree should primarily reinforce:

- Shadow damage
- Damage-over-time pressure
- Self-sustain
- Debuffs

Individual nodes remain to be designed.

## Cooldown Direction

The Shadow specialization may use class-wide cooldowns plus spec-specific cooldowns that reinforce its role.

The hero equips exactly two cooldown abilities at a time.

## Ultimate Direction

The Shadow specialization may have one or more compatible ultimate choices.

The hero equips exactly one ultimate at a time.

The chosen ultimate activates through the shared ultimate-bar system.

## Open Areas

- 3 Tier 1 talents
- 3 Tier 2 talents
- 2 Tier 3 capstones
- cooldown abilities
- ultimate abilities
- auto-attack timing and coefficient
- Mastery behavior
- ability priorities and targeting
