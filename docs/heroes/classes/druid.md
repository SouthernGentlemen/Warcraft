# Druid

## Resources

Druid uses Mana, Energy, or Rage depending on form.

| Form / State | Resource |
| --- | --- |
| Caster / Balance | Mana |
| Caster / Restoration | Mana |
| Cat Form | Energy |
| Bear Form | Rage |

## Specializations

| Specialization | Role Direction | Auto-Attack Direction | Primary Stat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Balance | Spell damage | Nature / Arcane spell auto-attack | Intellect |
| Feral | Melee damage or defense through form | Cat or Bear melee auto-attack | Agility |
| Restoration | Healing | Nature spell auto-attack | Intellect |

## Feral Forms

Feral is the specialization that most directly uses Druid's multi-resource model.

- Cat Form uses Energy and an agility-driven melee auto-attack.
- Bear Form uses Rage and a defensive melee auto-attack.

Whether a Feral hero can switch freely between Cat and Bear during combat or commits to a form in hero setup remains open.

## Talent Trees

Druid has three talent trees:

- Balance
- Feral
- Restoration

Each tree follows the shared 3-tier structure in [Classes](./README.md): 3 Tier 1 choices, 3 Tier 2 choices, and 2 mutually exclusive Tier 3 capstones.

## Combat Loadout

A Druid equips:

- the auto-attack defined by specialization and current form
- exactly 2 selected cooldown abilities
- exactly 1 selected ultimate

Ability costs use the resource associated with the Druid's current form.
