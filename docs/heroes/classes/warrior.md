# Warrior

## Resource

Rage

## Specializations

| Specialization | Role Direction | Auto-Attack Direction | Primary Stat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Arms | Heavy melee damage | Heavy weapon swing | Strength |
| Fury | Fast melee damage | Rapid weapon swing | Strength |
| Protection | Tank / defense | Defensive weapon strike | Strength |

## Talent Trees

Warrior has three talent trees:

- Arms
- Fury
- Protection

Each tree follows the shared 3-tier structure in [Classes](./README.md): 3 Tier 1 choices, 3 Tier 2 choices, and 2 mutually exclusive Tier 3 capstones.

## Combat Loadout

A Warrior equips:

- the auto-attack defined by the active specialization
- exactly 2 selected cooldown abilities
- exactly 1 selected ultimate

Warrior abilities spend Rage when they have a resource cost.

Rage is expected to be generated through combat rather than regenerated like Mana or Energy.
