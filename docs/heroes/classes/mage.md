# Mage

## Resource

Mana

## Specializations

| Specialization | Role Direction | Auto-Attack Direction | Primary Stat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Arcane | Spell damage / control | Arcane spell auto-attack | Intellect |
| Fire | Spell damage | Fire spell auto-attack | Intellect |
| Frost | Spell damage / control | Frost spell auto-attack | Intellect |

## Talent Trees

Mage has three talent trees:

- Arcane
- Fire
- Frost

Each tree follows the shared 3-tier structure in [Classes](./README.md): 3 Tier 1 choices, 3 Tier 2 choices, and 2 mutually exclusive Tier 3 capstones.

## Combat Loadout

A Mage equips:

- the auto-attack defined by the active specialization
- exactly 2 selected cooldown abilities
- exactly 1 selected ultimate

Mage abilities spend Mana when they have a resource cost.
