# Paladin

## Faction

Alliance only.

## Resource

Mana

## Specializations

| Specialization | Role Direction | Auto-Attack Direction | Primary Stat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Holy | Healing / support | Holy-infused attack | Intellect |
| Protection | Tank / defense | Defensive melee strike | Strength |
| Retribution | Melee damage | Holy melee strike | Strength |

## Talent Trees

Paladin has three talent trees:

- Holy
- Protection
- Retribution

Each tree follows the shared 3-tier structure in [Classes](./README.md): 3 Tier 1 choices, 3 Tier 2 choices, and 2 mutually exclusive Tier 3 capstones.

## Combat Loadout

A Paladin equips:

- the auto-attack defined by the active specialization
- exactly 2 selected cooldown abilities
- exactly 1 selected ultimate

Paladin abilities spend Mana when they have a resource cost.
