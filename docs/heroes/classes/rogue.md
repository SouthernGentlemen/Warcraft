# Rogue

## Resource

Energy

## Specializations

| Specialization | Role Direction | Auto-Attack Direction | Primary Stat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Assassination | Sustained melee damage | Fast precision melee strike | Agility |
| Combat | Direct melee damage | Weapon-focused melee strike | Agility |
| Subtlety | Burst / utility melee damage | Precision melee strike | Agility |

## Talent Trees

Rogue has three talent trees:

- Assassination
- Combat
- Subtlety

Each tree follows the shared 3-tier structure in [Classes](./README.md): 3 Tier 1 choices, 3 Tier 2 choices, and 2 mutually exclusive Tier 3 capstones.

## Combat Loadout

A Rogue equips:

- the auto-attack defined by the active specialization
- exactly 2 selected cooldown abilities
- exactly 1 selected ultimate

Rogue abilities spend Energy when they have a resource cost.
