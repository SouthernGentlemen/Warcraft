# Druid — Balance

## Identity

- Role: Ranged spell damage
- Resource: Mana
- Primary stat: Intellect
- Auto-attack: Wrath — ranged Nature/Arcane damage

## Talent Tree

The first talent point spent in this tree selects **Balance** as the hero's specialization.

All values below are starting implementation values and may be tuned during balance passes.

### Tier 1 — Level 1

Choose 1 of 3.

| Talent | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Lunar Empowerment** | Every third Wrath deals 30% additional damage. |
| **Solar Efficiency** | Mana costs of equipped cooldown abilities are reduced by 10%. |
| **Astral Drift** | Wrath's auto-attack bar fills 10% faster. |

### Tier 2 — Level 2

Requires a Tier 1 talent in this tree. Choose 1 of 3.

| Talent | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Eclipse** | Using an equipped cooldown empowers the next Wrath by 20%; Wrath then empowers the next equipped cooldown by 10%. |
| **Lunar Shower** | Critical Wraths generate 5% ultimate-bar charge. |
| **Nature's Grace** | Critical spell damage grants 15% Haste for 4 seconds. |

### Tier 3 — Level 3 Capstone

Requires a Tier 2 talent in this tree. Choose exactly 1 of 2.

| Capstone | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Celestial Alignment** | Using the selected ultimate grants 20% Spell Power and 20% Haste for 8 seconds. |
| **Starfall** | Every fifth Wrath also strikes all other enemies for 50% damage. |

Selecting a capstone completes the primary specialization path and unlocks the class's other two talent trees.

## Combat Loadout

This specialization uses its listed auto-attack, exactly 2 equipped cooldown abilities, and exactly 1 equipped ultimate.

Talents modify that shared combat package; they do not add extra active ability slots.
