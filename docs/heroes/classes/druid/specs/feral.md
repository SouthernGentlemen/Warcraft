# Druid — Feral

## Identity

- Role: Melee damage or defense through form
- Resource: Energy in Cat Form; Rage in Bear Form
- Primary stat: Agility
- Auto-attack: Cat Claw in Cat Form; Bear Swipe in Bear Form
