# Rogue — Combat

## Identity

- Role: Direct sustained melee damage
- Resource: Energy
- Primary stat: Agility
- Auto-attack: Weapon Strike — melee weapon damage

## Talent Tree

The first talent point spent in this tree selects **Combat** as the hero's specialization.

All values below are starting implementation values and may be tuned during balance passes.

### Tier 1 — Level 1

Choose 1 of 3.

| Talent | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Quick Draw** | Auto-attack bar fills 12% faster. |
| **Combat Potency** | Auto-attacks have a 20% chance to restore 5 Energy. |
| **Weapon Mastery** | Weapon Strike deals 12% more damage. |

### Tier 2 — Level 2

Requires a Tier 1 talent in this tree. Choose 1 of 3.

| Talent | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Blade Flurry** | Every fourth auto-attack also strikes a second enemy for 60% damage. |
| **Relentless Strikes** | Critical auto-attacks reduce both equipped cooldowns' remaining time by 1 second. |
| **Riposte** | After taking direct damage, the next auto-attack deals 50% additional damage; 8-second internal cooldown. |

### Tier 3 — Level 3 Capstone

Requires a Tier 2 talent in this tree. Choose exactly 1 of 2.

| Capstone | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Adrenaline Rush** | Using the selected ultimate grants 30% faster Energy regeneration and 20% auto-attack speed for 8 seconds. |
| **Killing Spree** | Every fifth auto-attack immediately triggers a second strike for 75% damage. |

Selecting a capstone completes the primary specialization path and unlocks the class's other two talent trees.

## Combat Loadout

This specialization uses its listed auto-attack, exactly 2 equipped cooldown abilities, and exactly 1 equipped ultimate.

Talents modify that shared combat package; they do not add extra active ability slots.
