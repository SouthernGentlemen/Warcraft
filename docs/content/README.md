# Content

## Purpose

Content is the primary way heroes gain experience, equipment, materials, and progression resources.

## Content Ladder

| Content | Heroes Required | First Tier |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Quest | 1 | 1 |
| Incursion | 1 | 1 |
| Assault | 3 | 2 |
| Dungeon | 5 | 3 |
| Raid | 10 | 4 |
| Siege | 20 | 5 |

The activity determines party size.

The tier determines difficulty and progression requirements.

## Quests

Single-hero assignments that support routine progression.

Expected uses include:

- experience
- gold
- common materials
- basic resources
- gathering
- scouting
- exploration

Quests exist from Tier 1 through Tier 5.

## Incursions

Single-hero combat-focused content.

Incursions exist from Tier 1 through Tier 5 and provide a more combat-oriented solo progression path than normal quests.

## Assaults

Three-hero content beginning at Tier 2.

Assaults introduce meaningful party composition before larger group content.

## Dungeons

Five-hero content beginning at Tier 3.

Dungeons are expected to contain multiple encounters and bosses and become an important source of equipment and profession materials.

## Raids

Ten-hero content beginning at Tier 4.

Raids represent large coordinated encounters and major progression rewards.

## Sieges

Twenty-hero Tier 5 content.

Sieges represent the largest planned deployment type and are intended to be a culmination of roster progression.

## Higher-Tier Versions

Unlocking a new content category does not invalidate older categories.

The intended availability is:

- Quest: Tiers 1–5
- Incursion: Tiers 1–5
- Assault: Tiers 2–5
- Dungeon: Tiers 3–5
- Raid: Tiers 4–5
- Siege: Tier 5

## Open Areas

Each content type still needs rules for:

- duration
- entry requirements
- enemy structure
- success and failure
- rewards
- repeatability
- bosses
- first-clear rewards
- difficulty scaling
- automation
