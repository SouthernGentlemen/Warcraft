# Crafting

## Purpose

Crafting converts gathered and rewarded materials into equipment, enhancements, and consumables that support hero and roster progression.

## Crafting Philosophy

Crafting should reinforce adventuring.

Content supplies materials and recipes. Crafting turns those resources into useful progression. Neither system should make the other unnecessary.

Professions progress through five tiers.

## Crafted Gear Quality

Crafted gear follows a fixed level-to-quality and stat-line progression.

| Level | Quality | Primary Stats | Tertiary Stats |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | White | 0 | 0 |
| 2 | Green | 1 | 0 |
| 3 | Blue | 2 | 0 |
| 4 | Epic | 2 | 1 |
| 5 | Epic | 2 | 2 |

The stat families themselves are defined in [Content / Stats](../content/stats/README.md).

This table defines stat-line count, not the numeric value of those stats.

## Blacksmith

Primary material progression:

1. Copper
2. Iron
3. Steel
4. Mithril
5. Thorium

Expected outputs include metal weapons, armor, shields, and components.

## Tailoring / Loom

Primary material progression:

1. Linen
2. Wool
3. Silk
4. Mageweave
5. Runecloth

Expected outputs include cloth equipment and related crafted components.

## Leatherworking

Leatherworking will use five corresponding leather tiers.

Exact material names and outputs still need to be defined.

Expected outputs include leather armor and related components.

## Enchanting

Enchanting uses tiered magical essences and corresponding shards.

Known direction:

- essences are a common magical crafting material
- shards are a corresponding higher-value material
- equipment may be broken down or otherwise converted into enchanting resources

Exact five-tier naming and enchantment rules remain open.

## Alchemy

Alchemy produces temporary consumables such as:

- potions
- flasks

Known reagent themes include:

- Air
- Water
- Earth
- Fire
- Undeath

Exact recipes, tiers, and effects remain open.

## Crafting Queues

Because the game is incremental, crafting is expected to interact with time and queues.

The exact relationship between crafting time, offline progression, building capacity, and automation will be specified later.

## Open Areas

Later crafting design must define:

- complete material lists
- recipes
- recipe unlocks
- crafting time
- output quantities
- profession buildings
- salvage
- disenchanting
- exact stat values
- crafted-stat selection rules
- crafting queues
- automation
