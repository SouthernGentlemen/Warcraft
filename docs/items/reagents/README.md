# Reagents

## Purpose

Reagents are profession-facing items used as inputs for crafting, enhancement, and item production.

They are distinct from equipment and from economy resources used for base or meta progression.

## Profession Ownership

Reagent families should map cleanly to the profession systems.

Known profession relationships include:

- Blacksmith — metals and forged-material inputs
- Alchemist — elemental and alchemical ingredients
- Enchanter — essences, shards, and enchanting materials
- Tailor — cloth materials
- Leatherworker — hides, leather, and related materials
- Engineer — components and technological materials

A reagent may be used by more than one profession when that creates useful interaction between systems.

## Tiering

Reagents should follow the five game tiers.

A reagent's tier determines the progression band of recipes and systems that consume it.

Individual professions may use different material names and acquisition sources within the same tier.

## Reagent Rules

Reagents are not hero equipment.

They may be:

- gathered or earned from activities
- produced by another profession or system
- consumed by recipes
- required in different quantities by different recipes
- used across multiple professions where appropriate

The exact stack, storage, trading, and inventory rules remain open.

## Design Goal

Reagents should make professions feel connected to progression without creating unnecessary inventory clutter.

Each reagent family should have a clear purpose, source, and sink.

## Open Areas

Later reagent design must define:

- exact reagent families
- five-tier material tables by profession
- acquisition sources
- recipe quantities
- cross-profession dependencies
- storage and stack behavior
- duplicate and overflow handling
